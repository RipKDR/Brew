# Brew — UA & Creative Strategy (2026)

## Ad Format Strategy

### Primary Formats

| Format          | Length / Type     | Share of Creative Volume | Purpose                              |
|-----------------|-------------------|--------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Short video     | 15 seconds        | 35%                      | Broad reach, scroll-stopping hooks   |
| Standard video  | 30 seconds        | 25%                      | Full gameplay loop demonstration     |
| Playable ad     | 20–30s interactive| 20%                      | Highest intent conversion            |
| UGC-style video | 15–30 seconds     | 15%                      | Authenticity, social proof           |
| Static / GIF    | Single frame      | 5%                       | Retargeting, low-bandwidth placements|

### Format-Specific Guidelines

**Video (15s/30s):**
- First 2 seconds are life or death. The hook must be visual AND auditory — never start with a logo or text card.
- Gameplay footage is captured at 60fps, slowed to 80% speed during brew moments for emphasis.
- End cards show App Store/Play Store badges, the Brew icon, and the tagline. Duration: 2 seconds max.
- All videos render with and without sound. Sound-off versions burn in captions and use text-based hooks.
- Aspect ratios: 9:16 (primary — TikTok, Reels, Stories), 1:1 (Feed), 4:5 (Meta Feed optimal). Every concept is shot/captured to work in all three crops.

**Playable:**
- Built in HTML5 (Luna, Playturbo, or equivalent)
- Frame rate: locked 30fps minimum on target devices
- Load time: under 3 seconds on 4G
- No tutorial text — gameplay should be self-explanatory with animated hand prompts

**UGC-style:**
- Front-facing camera framing, casual environment (couch, desk, bed)
- Creator speaks directly about the experience, not features
- Subtitles mandatory — 90% of impressions are sound-off
- Filmed by real users or UGC creators (not actors in a studio)
- Brief gameplay insert (3–5 seconds) showing the brew moment

## Video Creative Concepts

### Concept 1 — "One More Fusion"

**Duration:** 15s and 30s variants
**Hook (0–2s):** Close-up of a Tier 2 orb pulsing, almost brew-ready. A text overlay: "ONE away..." Steam rising.
**Body (2–12s):** The player scans the board. Finds a 3-token Ember cluster near the orb. Taps. Fusion fires. The orb grows to Tier 3 — steam, sparkles, glow. A beat of suspense. Then — BREW. Full animation. Sound at full blast.
**CTA (12–15s):** "That feeling? It never gets old." App icon + store badge.
**30s extension:** Before the successful brew, show a near-miss: the player taps a cluster that doesn't connect to the orb. Tension builds. Then they find the right path. Payoff is earned.
**Emotional core:** Tension and release. The almost-moment.

### Concept 2 — "Satisfying Chains"

**Duration:** 15s primary
**Hook (0–2s):** Black screen. A single fusion sound plays. Then a second, higher-pitched. Then a third. Each accompanied by a flash of ingredient color against black.
**Body (2–12s):** Smash cut to gameplay. A chain fusion cascades across the board — 4, 5, 6 fusions in sequence, each one triggering the next. Camera (virtual) follows the chain. No narration, no text. Just the escalating sound design and the visual cascade. Slow-motion on the final fusion.
**CTA (12–15s):** "Brew." One word. App icon.
**Emotional core:** ASMR for puzzle gamers. Pure audiovisual satisfaction.
**Note:** This concept should be A/B tested with different chain lengths and ingredient types. Ember chains (warm colors) are likely to perform best in cold-weather markets. Frost chains for warm-weather markets.

### Concept 3 — "Workshop Before / After"

**Duration:** 30s primary (15s condensed version)
**Hook (0–2s):** A sad, dusty, empty workshop. Cold. Grey. A single cobweb sways. Text: "Day 1."
**Body (2–22s):** Quick cuts of gameplay — 3 short brew moments (1.5 seconds each, compressed). Between each brew, the workshop transforms: fire lights, shelves fill, herbs appear, cat arrives. The transformation is satisfying — Tetris-clearing-lines level satisfying.
**Final (22–27s):** The full workshop at peak beauty. Warm, glowing, alive. The camera pulls back to reveal the potion shelf, overflowing with color. Text: "Day 30."
**CTA (27–30s):** "Build your workshop. Brew your world." App icon + store badge.
**Emotional core:** Transformation and ownership. Progress made visible.

### Concept 4 — "Choose Your Brew"

**Duration:** 30s
**Hook (0–3s):** Three potion recipes appear as cards, fanning out like a card game. Each has a distinct potion vial illustration and name: "Phoenix Tonic," "Moonbeam Elixir," "Verdant Draught." A hand taps "Moonbeam Elixir."
**Body (3–22s):** Gameplay focuses on collecting Frost and Sun orbs. The recipe tracker at the top fills in as each orb is brewed. Clear visual progress. The final brew triggers the Moonbeam Elixir — a special blue-gold potion vial materializes with unique particles.
**Close (22–27s):** The potion flies to the shelf. Camera follows. The shelf shows many potions already collected, with a few empty slots.
**CTA (27–30s):** "50+ potions to discover. What will you brew?" App icon.
**Emotional core:** Choice, creation, and collection. The player is the craftsperson.

### Concept 5 — "Can You Brew This?"

**Duration:** 15s (challenge-format, optimized for TikTok/Reels)
**Hook (0–2s):** Text overlay, bold: "CAN YOU BREW THE STARLIGHT ELIXIR?" An elaborate potion vial glitters on screen.
**Body (2–10s):** The board appears — complex, mid-game state. The recipe requires 2 Sun orbs and 1 Shadow orb. The player starts fusing. One Sun orb brews. Clock is ticking (move counter depleting). Second Sun orb almost ready... 1 move left...
**Cut (10s):** Screen freezes. Text: "Download to find out." Wink at the audience — the outcome is deliberately withheld.
**CTA (10–15s):** App icon + store badge. "Free to play."
**Emotional core:** Challenge and curiosity. The Zeigarnik effect — an unfinished task that nags until completed.

## Playable Ad Design

### Structure: 3-Step Tutorial Demo

**Step 1 — Fuse (5 seconds active play)**
- Board: simplified 5×5 grid with only Ember and Vine tokens
- A large 5-token Ember cluster is pre-positioned
- Animated hand prompt pulses on the cluster
- Player taps → fusion fires → Small Orb forms
- Text: "Tap clusters to fuse!"

**Step 2 — Chain (8 seconds active play)**
- Board resets: 6×6 grid, Sun and Frost tokens
- Two adjacent clusters are positioned so fusing one creates a chain reaction
- Hand prompt on the first cluster
- Player taps → first fusion → cascade → chain fusion fires
- Text: "Chain reactions = bigger orbs!"
- The resulting orb is Tier 2

**Step 3 — Brew (7 seconds active play + auto-brew)**
- The Tier 2 orb from Step 2 is on the board, plus a small cluster nearby
- Hand prompt on the cluster
- Player taps → fusion feeds the orb → orb reaches Tier 3 → auto-brews after 1 second hold
- Full brew animation plays (uninterrupted)
- The potion vial flies to a mini potion shelf at the top

**CTA Screen (auto-appears after brew)**
- Full-screen overlay: the brewed potion, glowing
- "You brewed your first potion!"
- Large "Download Free" button (Cauldron Amber, 56pt tall)
- App icon and store badges
- Background: workshop scene at upgrade level 30 (aspirational)

### Technical Requirements
- Canvas size: 320×568 minimum (scales up)
- Total asset weight: under 2MB (compressed)
- Audio: included but auto-muted (unmute on first interaction per platform policy)
- Fallback: if interaction times out after 5 seconds per step, auto-advance with simulated tap
- End card CTA must comply with MRAID 3.0 clickthrough standards

## Channel Strategy

### Budget Allocation

| Channel         | Budget % | Primary Format       | Optimization Target     |
|-----------------|----------|----------------------|--------------------------|
| Meta (FB + IG)  | 40%      | Video + Playable     | D7 ROAS, CPI             |
| TikTok          | 30%      | Short video, UGC     | CPI, IPM                 |
| Google UAC      | 20%      | All (auto-allocated) | tCPA, D1 retention       |
| Unity Ads       | 10%      | Playable + Video     | CPI, install volume      |

### Channel-Specific Notes

**Meta:**
- Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC) for broad reach with automated creative selection
- AEO campaigns optimizing for D1 retention events (not installs) once pixel has 50+ events/day
- Broad targeting — no interest stacking. Let Meta's algorithm find the audience with creative as the variable.
- Catalog creative: test dynamic product ads showing different potion types as "products" for retargeting

**TikTok:**
- Spark Ads (boosting organic creator content) for UGC concepts
- Smart+ campaigns with automated creative optimization
- Creative must feel native — no polished ads. Vertical, casual, sound-on design.
- Leverage trending audio formats where legally cleared. Layer game audio underneath.
- Creator partnerships: 10–15 micro-creators (10K–100K followers) in cozy/gaming/ASMR niches

**Google UAC:**
- Provide all asset types: video (landscape, portrait, square), images (1200×628, 1200×1200), HTML5 playable, headlines (5), descriptions (5)
- Optimize for tCPA with D1 open as the conversion event (switch to D7 ROAS once mature)
- Separate campaigns for Search, Display, and YouTube placements to isolate performance

**Unity Ads:**
- Playable ad as primary format — puzzle gamers engage deeply with interactive demos
- Rewarded video placements only (opt-in = higher quality installs)
- Cross-promote with complementary genres: match-3, merge, crafting, cozy games

## Creative Testing Cadence

### Weekly Cycle

| Day       | Activity                                                  |
|-----------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Monday    | Review prior week performance. Kill bottom 50% of creatives by CPI and D1 retention. |
| Tuesday   | Brief 5–10 new variants. Include: 2 new concepts, 3 iterations of winning concepts, 2–3 format/crop variants. |
| Wednesday | Creative production (internal or agency).                 |
| Thursday  | QA and upload. Launch all new variants into testing campaigns at $50–$100/day each. |
| Friday    | Monitor early signals. Pause any creative with IPM < 0.5% after 5K impressions. |
| Weekend   | Let campaigns run. Collect data.                          |

### Performance Thresholds (Kill / Scale)

| Metric            | Kill below      | Scale above     |
|-------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| IPM (Meta)        | < 1.0%          | > 2.5%          |
| CPI (iOS)         | > $3.00         | < $1.80         |
| CPI (Android)     | > $1.50         | < $0.80         |
| CTR (TikTok)      | < 0.8%          | > 2.0%          |
| Hook rate (3s)    | < 25%           | > 45%           |
| D1 retention      | < 35%           | > 45%           |

### Creative Fatigue

- Monitor frequency caps: creative fatigue typically onset at 3.5+ frequency on Meta
- Rotate top performers out after 14–21 days even if still performing — creative fatigue is real before metrics show it
- Every winning concept gets a "refresh": same structure, new ingredient colors / potion types / board states

| Platform | Soft Launch (Month 1–2) | Scaling (Month 3–6) | Mature (Month 7+) |

## App Store Optimization

### Keyword Strategy

**Primary keywords (highest volume, core relevance):**
- puzzle game
- potion craft
- brew game
- magic puzzle
- cozy game

**Secondary keywords (medium volume, strong relevance):**
- match and merge
- potion maker
- cauldron game
- crafting puzzle
- wizard puzzle

**Long-tail keywords (lower volume, high conversion intent):**
- cozy puzzle game relaxing
- potion brewing game
- magic workshop game
- satisfying puzzle game
- brew and craft

**Keyword placement priority:**
1. App name: "Brew: Potion Puzzle" (30 character limit, includes primary keyword)
2. Subtitle (iOS) / Short description (Android): "Fuse ingredients. Craft magic."
3. Keyword field (iOS, 100 chars): `puzzle,potion,brew,magic,cozy,craft,merge,cauldron,wizard,relaxing,satisfying,match,fusion,recipe`
4. Long description (Android): naturally integrate all primary and secondary keywords within the first 3 paragraphs

### Icon A/B Test Plan

**Test 1 (Launch):** Concept A (Bubbling Cauldron) vs. Concept C (Potion Vial)
- Run for 7 days minimum, 10K impressions per variant minimum
- Primary metric: CVR (conversion rate from impression to install)
- Secondary: tap-through rate on search results

**Test 2 (Post-launch week 3):** Winner of Test 1 vs. a color variant (swap background from amber to deep purple)
- Tests whether dark backgrounds improve visibility in the store grid

**Test 3 (Month 2):** Winner of Test 2 vs. Concept B (Glowing Orb) — test the abstract option against the proven winner
- If Concept B wins, it suggests the audience responds to mystery over clarity

### Screenshot A/B Test Plan

**Test 1:** Current order (Gameplay → Brew → Recipe → Workshop → Shelf) vs. reversed lead (Workshop → Brew → Gameplay → Recipe → Shelf)
- Hypothesis: leading with the aspirational workshop may hook casual players who aren't immediately drawn to puzzle gameplay

**Test 2:** With vs. without device frames
- Current best practice is frameless, but test to confirm

**Test 3:** Text-heavy captions vs. minimal text (2–3 words per screenshot)
- Hypothesis: shorter captions allow the visuals to sell the game
