## Full Description

Welcome to your workshop.

A cozy corner of the world where ingredients shimmer, cauldrons bubble, and every tap brings you closer to brewing something wonderful.

TAP TO FUSE
Tap clusters of matching ingredients to fuse them into glowing orbs. The bigger the cluster, the more powerful the orb. Watch them pulse, glow, and grow.

CHAIN REACTIONS
Set off cascading fusions that ripple across the board. Plan your moves or let happy accidents create spectacular chains. Every chain feels like magic.

BREW POTIONS
When an orb is ready, it transforms into a beautiful potion — with bubbling, sparkling, deeply satisfying animations. Collect 50+ unique recipes from Phoenix Tonic to Moonbeam Elixir.

BUILD YOUR WORKSHOP
Start with a dusty room. End with the coziest magical workshop in the land. Every potion you brew brings new shelves, decorations, a crackling fire, and maybe even a sleepy cat.

FEATURES
- Simple to learn, endlessly satisfying to master
- 50+ potion recipes to discover and brew
- A living workshop that grows with your collection
- Daily brew challenges and win streak rewards
- Weekly events with exclusive potions
- No pressure — play at your own pace
- Beautiful hand-crafted art and soothing music

Your cauldron is warm. Your ingredients are waiting.
